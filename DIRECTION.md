# Direction

## Current pressure

AoA needs to delegate bounded work to model configurations chosen for the task
without turning model reputation into folklore. The first pressure is GPT-5.6
Luna for landing readiness, bounded landing preparation, independent review,
post-landing verification, and closeout.

## Current horizon

1. Establish exact Luna `max` and `xhigh` read-only and bounded
   workspace-write realization records, with external effects disabled.
2. Prove claim lifecycle, source/derived separation, and unsupported-promotion
   rejection locally.
3. Keep exact Codex realization currentness tied to live runtime/catalog facts
   for one immutable `runtime_subject` source/digest, rather than a calendar
   review date or named runtime version alone.
4. Serve the first real consumer through a bounded, content-addressed read-only
   property query, then bind that exact fit evidence through an `aoa-sdk`
   incarnation and an `abyss-stack` external Codex process without owning
   either.
5. Feed fixed-input Sol/Luna landing studies to `aoa-evals` for independent
   comparison and verdict meaning.
6. Admit only the task and effort boundary supported by net-benefit evidence.
7. Use the first evidence-complete `eval`, `stats`, and `memo` duties as
   operational canaries for separate Luna fit hypotheses; do not require a
   sterile benchmark campaign before useful work, and do not promote one
   successful duty into general role fit.

The study contour measures actual tokens, active wall time, turns, output,
commands, review burden, and rework. It does not prescribe token, time, turn,
output, or cost budgets that terminate an agent run before those observations
exist. Provider-enforced limits and explicit operator interruption are retained
as external evidence. Any later operational guardrail must be derived from
measurements and evaluated separately from model capability.

## Deferred, not rejected

- internal representation and causal-intervention studies;
- model character and temperament research across time and environments;
- richer longitudinal model lineage;
- richer access or storage planes beyond the current bounded read-only fit
  query;
- flexible A2A relations among persistent role-bearing agents.

These directions remain open. The current schemas are a first observation
contour, not a final ontology of model interiors or artificial individuality.

## Growth triggers

Expand only when concrete evidence creates pressure:

- add an evidence modality implementation when a reproducible study can
  populate it;
- add a new realization dimension when two otherwise identical observations
  diverge because that dimension was missing;
- add an access plane when at least one bounded consumer cannot use the source
  or generated artifact route safely;
- revise or supersede a claim when counterevidence changes its useful scope.
